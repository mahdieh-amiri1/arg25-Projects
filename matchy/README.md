## Project Description
**Matchy** is a location-based AI networking platform that helps people discover and connect with others nearby who share similar interests, goals, or needs.  
It’s designed for **events, co-working spaces, universities, or any social environment** where participants want to find more relevant people to meet.

Users connect their wallet and must hold an **onchain passport** (e.g., [app.passport.xyz](https://app.passport.xyz)) to verify their humanity score and ensure they are real individuals.  
After verification, each user creates a lightweight profile containing their interests, goals, and social handles (X, Farcaster, etc.).  

An **AI matchmaker** analyzes these profiles and suggests compatible matches within the same physical area.  
When a match is found, **both users receive a notification** and can view limited public data from each other’s profile to decide whether to connect.  
If both accept, a chat channel opens so they can coordinate and meet in person.

**Future roadmap:**  
- Add privacy-preserving features using **Zero-Knowledge proofs (ZK)** to verify user attributes without revealing underlying data.  
- Integrate with **event platforms like Luma** so organizers can activate Matchy during networking sessions or breaks.  
- Introduce **gamification and badges** to reward engagement and participation.  
- Evolve into a **fun, secure, and privacy-first networking experience** powered by verifiable AI and decentralized identity.
